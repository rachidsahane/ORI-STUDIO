# expected-runs.txt: what must have run, and how recently. The declaration half
# of the liveness record. The other half is run-history.txt. README.md at the
# repository root gives the grammar and says what a gate does with both.
#
# One record per line. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
#
#   expected workflow=<path> required=<true|false> window_seconds=<integer>
#
# Read plainly: the workflow at <path>, relative to the repository root, is
# required to have executed at least once inside every window of
# <window_seconds> seconds. 604800 seconds is seven days.
expected workflow=.github/workflows/ci.yml required=true window_seconds=604800
