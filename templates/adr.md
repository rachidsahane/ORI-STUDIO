# Architecture decision record

One significant design decision, recorded when it is made. It also defines what must be escalated in its area from then on.

Source: AICD appendix A.4, reproduced field for field. Nothing is added.

## Fields

| Field | Required | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Title and identifier | Yes | Example: ADR-012: Event store for order history |
| Status | Yes | Proposed, accepted, superseded (by), deprecated |
| Context | Yes | The forces at play: requirements, constraints, what prompted the decision |
| Options considered | Yes | Each option with its trade-offs |
| Decision | Yes | What was chosen and why |
| Consequences | Yes | What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what the agents must now respect |
| Escalation scope | Yes | Which changes in this area must be escalated to a human |
